Day trips

What is within a tank of fuel

Yellowstone National Park

About 2 hours north on Highway 20 to the West Entrance. Idaho Falls is the closest major city to that gate, which is why so many people base here instead of fighting for a pad in a gateway town.

Grand Teton & Jackson Hole

90 minutes to two hours east, depending on your route and where in the park you are headed. Jackson Hole sits just beyond it, under 100 miles out.

Craters of the Moon

A lava field the Apollo astronauts trained on, roughly 90 minutes west. Strange, stark, and completely unlike everything else within a day of here.

Island Park & Sun Valley

Island Park sits between here and Yellowstone's west side; Sun Valley is a longer but very worthwhile run west. Both are comfortable day trips from a seasonal site.

These four cards reuse park photography. Your media library has no Yellowstone or Teton imagery in it, and nothing here pretends otherwise — licensed shots of the west entrance, the Tetons and Jackson would make this the strongest page on the site, because it is the search everyone actually runs.
In town

Two miles to downtown, zero to the trail

The Greenbelt starts at our fence — fourteen paved miles along the Snake River, which you can walk, run or ride on one of our beach cruisers.

Follow it and you reach the waterfalls the city is named for, the River Walk, and a downtown with museums, a zoo, a farmers market and enough restaurants to fill a week. The river itself is open for fishing, kayaking and paddle boarding.

  • Idaho Falls River Walk and the falls themselves
  • Museum of Idaho and the Idaho Falls Zoo
  • Farmers market and downtown festivals in season
  • Melaleuca Field — minor league baseball on a summer night
Where to eat

Dinner is five minutes away

The big week

4th of July in Idaho Falls

Idaho Falls puts its fireworks up over the Snake River, and the park fills months in advance for the holiday week.

Book early — and note that the holiday carries its own cancellation window: cancel 30 days prior for a refund, less the $10 transaction fee.
